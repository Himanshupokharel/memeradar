MEMERADAR — USER-PERSPECTIVE PLATFORM WORKFLOW

OVERVIEW

MemeRadar is an AI-native memecoin intelligence platform designed to help traders discover promising tokens earlier, understand why they are moving, avoid manipulated or dangerous setups, receive evidence-backed Buy/Sell signals, and monitor positions continuously after entry.

Instead of forcing users to manually combine DEX Screener, wallet trackers, holder analytics, risk tools, and trading terminals, MemeRadar brings the important information into one decision-making workflow.


GOALS

1. Help users discover potentially strong memecoin opportunities before they become obviously trending.
2. Reduce avoidable losses through structural risk, creator, holder, wallet, liquidity, and manipulation analysis.
3. Give users clear and explainable Buy, Hold, Caution, Take Profit, and Sell guidance instead of overwhelming them with raw data.
4. Monitor trades continuously after entry and warn users when the original thesis changes.
5. Build a transparent historical record showing how MemeRadar signals actually performed.
6. Eventually allow users to research, monitor, and execute trades without leaving MemeRadar.
